Silver jubilee · October 2026

Twenty-five years of Pala’s kitchens.

25 വർഷം. ഞങ്ങളോടൊപ്പം നിന്നതിന് നന്ദി!

This October, Jubilee Indane Home turns 25. Gigi James opened the office in 2001. Since then it has grown to about 14,500 homes and 600 businesses. Thank you to every family, shop and kitchen that has cooked with us.
