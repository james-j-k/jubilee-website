# Copywriting

## Voice

Calm, direct, and capable. Jubilee speaks as a well-run service, not an advertiser. It makes one promise at a time, explains what happens next, and avoids unsupported scale claims.

## Message hierarchy

| Moment | Copy job | Direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Home hero | State the fundamental value. | “Reliable LPG delivery, thoughtfully managed.” |
| Hero support | Add useful context without a claim that needs proof. | “Everyday support for homes and businesses that depend on it.” |
| Reliability System | Explain the operational model. | “A clearer route from request to support.” |
| Services | Help a visitor choose. | “Support for the moments that keep home moving.” |
| Commercial | Signal a distinct offer. | “LPG support built around your operation.” |
| Safety | Make care actionable. | “Know what to do, before you need to.” |
| Contact | Reduce friction. | “Start with the route that works for you.” |

## Calls to action

Use precise, low-pressure verbs: `Contact us`, `Explore services`, `Discuss commercial supply`, `View safety guidance`, and `Call support`. CTA labels describe the next interaction; they never use vague labels such as `Learn more` as the only action.

## Claims policy

“Trusted by thousands of families” may be restored only after the count, geography, time period, and owner are documented. Until then, the approved hero is the primary message above. This change is intentional: premium trust is earned through specificity, not unverified scale.
