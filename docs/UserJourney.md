# User Journeys

## Journey principles

Every journey starts with an intent and ends with an actionable next step. There are no dead ends, ornamental detours, or forced account flows. Contact is deliberately available as a direct call option and a considered form because urgency and preference differ by visitor.

## 1. Household customer seeking service

| Stage | Visitor need | Experience | Design decision and rationale |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Arrival | Know whether this is relevant and credible. | Hero promise, concise context, primary contact action. | A strong first screen limits cognitive load and avoids a generic agency introduction. |
| Orientation | Find the relevant service. | Service overview with clear labels and short outcomes. | Outcome-led cards reduce terminology barriers. |
| Fit | Confirm Jubilee can help. | Approved service-area/availability detail or a concise “confirm your area” path. | Fit information before a long form prevents frustration and improves enquiry quality. |
| Evaluation | Confirm reliability. | Metrics and proof points near the service path. | Evidence appears before, not after, the decision point. |
| Action | Ask for help. | Contact panel with call and short form options. | A call is faster for urgent needs; a form supports quieter, considered enquiries. |
| Recovery | Correct an issue without losing work. | Inline errors, retained form values, direct alternative contact. | A failed submission must not create a dead end. |
| Confirmation | Know what happens next. | Clear submitted state and only operations-approved response guidance. | Explicit, truthful expectations reduce anxiety and duplicate enquiries. |

## 2. Commercial decision-maker

| Stage | Visitor need | Experience | Design decision and rationale |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Arrival | Assess business relevance quickly. | Commercial navigation item and home-page commercial feature. | Commercial visitors should not need to infer that domestic service scales to their operation. |
| Qualification | Understand fit. | Benefits, support model, and enquiry criteria. | Transparent qualification saves both visitor and team time. |
| Confidence | De-risk a relationship. | Operational approach, safety commitment, and contact route. | Commercial buyers value dependable process over consumer-style promotions. |
| Action | Start a serious conversation. | Focused commercial enquiry form. | The form collects only information required to route the enquiry; long forms damage completion. |

## 3. Safety-conscious or urgent visitor

| Stage | Visitor need | Experience | Design decision and rationale |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Arrival | Get help with no ambiguity. | Persistent contact action plus a clearly named Safety route. | Safety must be discoverable globally, not buried in a footer. |
| Guidance | Find the right instruction. | Scannable, task-based safety sections and accordions. | Chunking supports stressed readers and small screens. |
| Escalation | Reach a human when needed. | Prominent emergency/support contact treatment. | The design never implies that a website is a substitute for urgent help. |

## Journey safeguards

- Navigation never hides contact information behind a menu on desktop; on mobile a labelled call action remains in the header.
- Forms use plain labels, inline validation, and no nonessential fields.
- If a user abandons a form, no dark pattern, forced account, or modal intercept is used; direct contact remains available.
- Success states never claim an exact response time unless operations have approved it.
- Motion does not delay emergency information or block interaction.

The result feels considered like a premium product flow, while respecting the real-world stakes of fuel supply and safety.
