# Content Strategy

## Content job

Content must reduce uncertainty: what Jubilee supports, how the experience works, whether the visitor is a fit, and how to take the next step. It is not filler for visual sections.

## Narrative pillars

1. **Dependable everyday energy:** the household promise, stated plainly.
2. **A system behind the service:** only approved operational process and support detail.
3. **Safety as care:** useful, practical guidance without alarming language or medical/legal improvisation.
4. **Commercial continuity:** a distinct, qualified proposition for operations that depend on LPG.

## Source-of-truth rules

- Operations owns service scope, hours, service areas, emergency contact, and response expectations.
- Marketing owns voice and approved proof; all claims need a source and review date.
- Testimonials require name/context permission and attribution approval.
- Metrics require source, period, calculation method, and a refresh owner; otherwise omit them.
- Safety content requires approval by the responsible operational or regulatory owner and a review date.

## Page jobs

Home orients and directs. Services explains household choices. Commercial qualifies a business conversation. Safety gives task-led guidance and an escalation route. About makes the people and operating standard credible. Contact removes friction.

## Editorial constraints

Write in short, concrete sentences. Lead with outcomes, use real nouns and verbs, and avoid “best,” “leading,” “seamless,” “world-class,” “trusted by thousands,” and similar claims unless substantiated. Each section needs one conclusion and one next step.
