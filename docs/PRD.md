# Jubilee Website PRD

## Product objective

Create a high-trust, premium mobile-first web experience that helps household and commercial visitors understand Jubilee’s LPG support, confirm basic fit, access safety guidance, and start the right conversation with minimal friction.

## Audiences and jobs

| Audience | Primary job | Success moment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Household customer | Find relevant LPG help and contact support confidently. | Calls or sends a correctly routed enquiry. |
| Commercial decision-maker | Determine whether Jubilee can support an operation. | Starts a qualified commercial conversation. |
| Safety visitor | Find clear, approved guidance and the correct escalation path. | Acts on guidance or reaches support without delay. |
| Evaluator | Verify who Jubilee is and why it is dependable. | Moves to services, commercial, or contact with informed confidence. |

## Goals

- Make household, commercial, and safety paths obvious within the first interaction.
- Establish credibility through approved proof, process clarity, and visual quality.
- Deliver a fast, accessible mobile experience that feels designed, not templated.
- Capture enquiries with only the information required to respond usefully.

## Non-goals

- Online ordering, payment, account management, delivery tracking, or live availability claims.
- Invented customer testimonials, counters, service coverage, or regulatory statements.
- Decorative trends that compromise task completion or performance.

## Success measures

Define baseline and targets only after analytics consent, business capacity, and event taxonomy are approved. Candidate measures are: contact start rate, completed enquiry rate, call intent taps, commercial-enquiry quality, safety-route discovery, performance Core Web Vitals, and accessibility defects.

## Dependencies before launch

Approved company name/logo, service area, phone and email contacts, office hours, commercial qualification criteria, service list, safety content owner, privacy/legal content, authentic visual assets, and evidence sources. Missing operational facts must result in conditional UI, not placeholder claims.

## Acceptance criteria

All six routes are usable at 320px and desktop; every page exposes a clear next step; direct call information is reachable on mobile; the contact form validates accessibly; all images have an approved purpose and alt decision; motion respects reduced-motion settings; and no claim is published without an accountable source.
