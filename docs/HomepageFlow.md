# Homepage Flow — Jubilee Indane Home

## Narrative premise

The homepage is a single trust-building sequence, not a stack of marketing modules. Its job is to move a visitor from national recognition to local preference:

```text
Recognise the ecosystem
        ↓
Recognise Jubilee as the local institution
        ↓
Understand what makes Jubilee the better choice
        ↓
Find the right kind of support
        ↓
Confirm care, proof, and next step
        ↓
Contact Jubilee Indane Home
```

IndianOil and Indane establish legitimacy only in the Hero endorsement rail. Every later section is owned by Jubilee Indane Home. This preserves the endorsed relationship while making Jubilee—not Indane—the remembered identity.

## Global framing: not a content section

The public-site header is deliberately restrained. On desktop it contains the Jubilee Indane Home lockup, literal route labels, and one `Contact` recovery link. On mobile it contains the Jubilee lockup, a labelled direct-call action once approved, and a menu.

Official IndianOil and Indane marks are not repeated in the header after the Hero. Repetition would flatten the hierarchy and turn institutional endorsement into the page’s dominant brand. They may reappear only in a legally required footer or ownership statement, using approved assets unchanged.

## Page sequence

| Order | Chapter | Narrative movement | Primary conversion state |
| ---: | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | The Hero: The Jubilee Standard | “This is an authorised, credible local distributor.” | I know who I am dealing with. |
| 2 | Local Accountability | “This agency is part of my town, not a distant service label.” | I believe Jubilee is relevant to me. |
| 3 | The Jubilee Difference | “Jubilee has a professional standard beyond the minimum.” | I have a reason to prefer Jubilee over another distributor. |
| 4 | Support, Clearly Routed | “I can see the path that fits my household or business.” | I know where my need belongs. |
| 5 | Care Beyond the Cylinder | “Safety and responsible support are built into the relationship.” | I feel respected, not merely sold to. |
| 6 | Proof That Belongs to Jubilee | “The choice is backed by real, local evidence.” | I am ready to stop comparing. |
| 7 | Speak to Jubilee | “There is a direct, low-friction next step.” | I contact Jubilee Indane Home. |

Each transition is a response to the question naturally created by the chapter before it. No section should independently restate the Hero, introduce a new visual system, or compete for primary CTA attention.

## 1. The Hero — The Jubilee Standard

### Customer question

**“Who are you, are you legitimate, and can I trust you with my LPG support?”**

### Why it exists

The Hero establishes the three-layer trust model in one first screen:

1. Official IndianOil and Indane recognition.
2. Jubilee’s authorised-distributor relationship.
3. Jubilee Indane Home as the professional local choice.

### Business goal

Create immediate brand recognition, reduce comparison behaviour, and provide the fastest possible route to contact.

### Content

- Official IndianOil and Indane assets on a solid, legally approved endorsement rail.
- Approved relationship statement: `Authorised IndianOil Indane Distributor`.
- Jubilee Indane Home as the dominant H1 identity.
- Approved locality statement, or the no-locality fallback.
- One direct CTA: `Contact Jubilee Indane Home`.
- The Jubilee Standard plaque with the no-image/no-proof fallback until approved content exists.

### Never include

- A second dominant CTA.
- A carousel, scroll cue, stock cylinder image, fake operational dashboard, or map.
- Unverified delivery promises, ratings, customer counts, or response times.
- Altered, glassified, animated, recoloured, or recreated IndianOil/Indane marks.

### Transition to the next chapter

The Hero earns institutional trust. The next question is not “what other products do you have?” It is: **“Is Jubilee genuinely part of my community and accountable to it?”**

## 2. Local Accountability — Jubilee, Here for [Town]

### Customer question

**“Is this a real local agency that understands and serves my area?”**

### Why it exists

IndianOil and Indane prove the national ecosystem; this chapter proves Jubilee’s local relevance. It is the first moment where the visitor sees the agency as a nearby institution rather than a logo relationship.

### Business goal

Qualify visitors by approved service area and make Jubilee feel locally accountable—particularly when nearby authorised distributors offer the same underlying Indane product.

### Content

- An outcome-led heading: `Jubilee, here for [approved town or service area].`
- One short, sourced paragraph describing the agency’s approved local role or history.
- One authentic visual: the real agency frontage, service counter, office material, local landmark, or approved operational detail. The image must be clearly local and honestly captioned where context is not obvious.
- Optional factual location block: approved address/locality, only if this is ready for publication.
- A text-level link to `About Jubilee` or `Contact Jubilee`; do not introduce a second large CTA.

### Never include

- Generic “we are your trusted local partner” copy without a fact, place, or real visual to support it.
- A generic stock family, a pin on an inaccurate map, or an over-detailed area list that creates service promises.
- IndianOil/Indane logos again; Jubilee owns this chapter.
- A wall of photos, founder biography, or decorative local montage.

### Transition to the next chapter

Local presence earns relevance but not preference. The next question becomes: **“Why should I choose Jubilee instead of another authorised Indane distributor nearby?”**

## 3. The Jubilee Difference — A Standard You Can Feel

### Customer question

**“What does Jubilee do better for me?”**

### Why it exists

This is the differentiation chapter. It translates “professional and reliable” from adjectives into a small number of observable, approved service principles.

### Business goal

Give visitors a specific reason to prefer Jubilee while avoiding generic claims that every distributor could make.

### Content

Use exactly three operational principles, each approved by the responsible business owner. The final labels must describe a real customer experience, for example:

1. `Clear support` — how customers receive useful next-step information.
2. `Local accountability` — what makes the agency reachable and responsible locally.
3. `Care in the details` — a verified support or safety practice.

Each principle contains:

- A 2–4 word title.
- One concise, substantiated explanation (maximum 25 words).
- A small structural visual or line marker, never an illustrated icon set.

The three principles are connected by the restrained Jubilee energy line introduced in the Hero. This makes the page feel like one system without repeating the Hero plaque.

### Never include

- Generic values such as “quality,” “trust,” “excellence,” or “customer-first” without an observable meaning.
- Feature-card grids, animated counters, badges, or floating glass cards.
- More than three principles; additional items weaken comprehension and make the agency sound defensive.
- Process claims that operations cannot validate.

### Transition to the next chapter

Once a visitor understands the Jubilee standard, their question becomes practical: **“Can this standard help with what I need today?”**

## 4. Support, Clearly Routed — Home or Business

### Customer question

**“Which Jubilee support path is right for me?”**

### Why it exists

The visitor should not navigate a dense list of LPG services or infer whether commercial needs are treated seriously. This chapter turns trust into an intelligible choice.

### Business goal

Route household and commercial enquiries accurately, reduce low-quality contacts, and make the commercial proposition feel deliberate rather than appended.

### Content

Use two unequal but balanced paths:

#### Home support

- Heading: `For your home.`
- A concise approved description of household support.
- Two or three approved household service outcomes.
- Text action: `Explore home support`.

#### Commercial support

- Heading: `For the work that keeps moving.`
- A concise approved description of commercial LPG support.
- Two or three approved commercial use-case/outcome statements.
- Text action: `Discuss commercial support`.

The composition is an editorial split, not two equal marketing cards. On mobile it becomes a sequential narrative: Home first, then Commercial, each with an obvious route.

### Never include

- A catalogue of every product SKU, refill price, cylinder weight, or form field.
- A comparison table that implies household and commercial support are interchangeable.
- Unapproved availability, turnaround, inventory, or price claims.
- Button overload; each path has one text action only.

### Transition to the next chapter

After choosing a path, a thoughtful visitor asks: **“Will this distributor treat safety and responsibility as part of the service?”**

## 5. Care Beyond the Cylinder — Safety Is Part of the Standard

### Customer question

**“Does Jubilee take responsibility beyond the transaction?”**

### Why it exists

Safety is not a compliance footnote for LPG. Presenting it calmly demonstrates care, professionalism, and long-term accountability without making the homepage alarming.

### Business goal

Build trust, direct safety-conscious visitors to approved guidance, and reduce avoidable support ambiguity.

### Content

- Heading: `Safety is part of the Jubilee Standard.`
- One approved, non-alarmist explanatory sentence.
- Three short, approved safety topics, for example: `Before you use LPG`, `Everyday checks`, and `When you need help`.
- One text action: `View safety guidance`.
- A clearly presented approved emergency/support route where required by operations and applicable guidance.

Use a quiet warm-neutral surface with a fine Jubilee red rule. It should feel composed and practical, not like a warning banner.

### Never include

- Unreviewed safety advice, DIY repair instructions, sensational imagery, or flame graphics.
- A fake emergency control, scrolling alert, or animated urgency.
- A full FAQ in the homepage; route detailed guidance to the dedicated Safety page.
- Official IndianOil/Indane logo repetition.

### Transition to the next chapter

Care establishes values; the next question is: **“What real evidence confirms that Jubilee lives up to this standard?”**

## 6. Proof That Belongs to Jubilee

### Customer question

**“What evidence tells me Jubilee is the right local choice?”**

### Why it exists

This is the final comparison-stopping moment. It must prove Jubilee specifically—not borrow scale from IndianOil or make vague claims about Indane.

### Business goal

Give high-intent visitors the evidence required to contact Jubilee rather than compare neighbouring distributors.

### Content

Render exactly one approved evidence format. The format is chosen by the strongest available proof, not by a desire to fill space:

1. **Local tenure:** `[X] years serving [Town]` with source and review owner.
2. **Attributed customer statement:** under 18 words, with name/context permission.
3. **Verified agency fact:** one specific, publishable operational credential.

The evidence should sit beside a small `Jubilee Indane Home` nameplate and a concise explanatory line. This remains a Jubilee chapter; the IndianOil/Indane endorsement is not repeated.

If no evidence has been approved, remove this chapter entirely and increase the transition space before the final contact chapter. An empty proof section is worse than none.

### Never include

- Star-rating mosaics, logo walls, scrolling review carousels, count-up metrics, or unverified consumer quotes.
- IndianOil national network statistics presented as Jubilee achievements.
- More than one proof type at a time.
- A made-up “customer success” narrative.

### Transition to the next chapter

The visitor has enough context and proof. The only remaining question is: **“What is the simplest way to reach Jubilee now?”**

---

## 7. Speak to Jubilee — A Direct Route to Help

### Customer question

**“What do I do next, and will a real person help me?”**

### Why it exists

The closing conversion moment is where the page converts trust into a direct relationship. It should feel like an invitation from Jubilee, not an aggressive lead-capture form.

### Business goal

Maximise qualified contact while offering a lower-friction route for urgent or phone-first visitors.

### Content

- Heading: `Speak to Jubilee Indane Home.`
- Supporting line: `Choose the route that works best for you.`
- Primary CTA: `Contact Jubilee Indane Home`.
- Direct phone action, only once the number is approved and operable.
- Optional concise expectation statement only if approved, e.g. how an enquiry is routed—not an unsupported response-time promise.
- A quiet repetition of the `Jubilee Indane Home` nameplate; no official logos here.

On mobile, direct call appears before the form route if the contact is urgent. On desktop, the contact action is visually dominant, with the direct phone action secondary.

### Never include

- Long forms, forced account creation, pop-ups, chat widgets, countdowns, or “limited slots” language.
- An unsupported response-time guarantee.
- Generic `Submit` or `Get started` labels.
- A final visual that competes with the contact action.

## Continuous-story design rules

### Ownership of brand signals

| Page zone | Brand that leads | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hero endorsement rail | IndianOil and Indane | Immediate ecosystem recognition. |
| Hero headline and CTA | Jubilee Indane Home | Ensures Jubilee becomes the memory. |
| Every chapter after Hero | Jubilee Indane Home | Converts inherited legitimacy into local preference. |
| Legal/footer context only | IndianOil and Indane, where approved | Preserves endorsement without visual dominance. |

### Rhythm and visual continuity

- Alternate open editorial stages with structured information panels: Hero stage → local stage → principle panel → support stage → safety panel → evidence stage → contact stage.
- The Jubilee energy line may reappear once between the Local Accountability and Jubilee Difference chapters, then once more as a small connector in the final contact chapter. It never becomes a decorative page-long animation.
- Use mineral canvas as the continuous base. Surface changes should express a narrative change, never simply decorate a new section.
- Typography becomes progressively more practical as the page progresses: brand promise → local story → service clarity → direct contact.
- IndianOil/Indane colors remain reserved for official assets. Jubilee’s restrained operational red provides the page’s connective accent.

### CTA rhythm

- Hero: one primary contact CTA.
- Local Accountability: one understated informational link.
- Jubilee Difference: no CTA; this is a comprehension moment.
- Support routes: one text action per path.
- Safety: one text action.
- Evidence: no CTA; evidence should not feel like a sales interruption.
- Closing chapter: one primary contact CTA and one approved direct-call path.

This spacing prevents every section from asking for a conversion and makes the final contact action feel earned.

## Content governance and conditional rendering

| Content type | Requirement before publishing | If unavailable |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Official marks | Approved original assets and usage permission | Retain a legally reviewed relationship text treatment only; do not recreate marks. |
| Locality/service area | Operations-approved wording | Use the neutral fallback; do not infer coverage. |
| Service descriptions | Operations-approved service scope | Omit the unapproved route or use general contact only. |
| Safety content | Approved operational/regulatory owner and review date | Link only to approved external/support guidance; do not improvise. |
| Evidence | Source, date, owner, and publication approval | Remove the entire Evidence chapter. |
| Contact details | Current, monitored destination | Do not launch the final CTA or display a disabled control. |

## Homepage acceptance criteria

- [ ] A visitor can name `Jubilee Indane Home` after viewing the Hero and at least two later chapters.
- [ ] Official IndianOil and Indane recognition appears in the Hero only, unless legally required elsewhere.
- [ ] Every chapter answers the next natural customer question created by the previous chapter.
- [ ] Every customer claim is owned by Jubilee or appropriately attributed; no IndianOil scale claim is implied to be Jubilee’s.
- [ ] The page contains no generic feature grid, testimonial carousel, stock-image collage, decorative counters, floating chat, or repeated logo wall.
- [ ] Household, commercial, safety, and contact routes are visible without making all visitors process every path.
- [ ] The final contact chapter is the strongest post-Hero conversion moment and repeats Jubilee Indane Home by name.
