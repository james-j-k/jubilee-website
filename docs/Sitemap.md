| Route | URL | Role | Required cross-links |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Home | `/` | Orient, establish the promise, and route intent. | Services, Commercial, Safety, Contact. |
| About | `/about` | Explain the people, standard, and approved proof behind Jubilee. | Services and Contact. |

The sitemap remains intentionally shallow: a visitor can understand the entire product surface without dropdowns or search. Every route includes header, footer, safety discovery, and an appropriate next action.
