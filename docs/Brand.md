# Brand Identity — Quiet Energy

## Core idea

Jubilee should feel like **Quiet Energy**: essential energy delivered with composure, precision, and care. The brand does not try to make LPG glamorous. It makes a consequential everyday service feel exceptionally well run.

This is the distinction between a premium product experience and a polished local-business site. Premium comes from clarity, editing, tactile detail, and dependable behavior—not excessive red, promotional badges, or visual noise.

## Personality

- **Assured, never loud:** state what is known; avoid hype and urgency theatre.
- **Warmly exact:** human language paired with disciplined layouts and operational detail.
- **Quietly capable:** show the system behind the service rather than making vague promises.
- **Safety-conscious:** calm is not casual; safety is clear, practical, and easy to reach.

## Visual signature

A fine “energy line” is the only recurring expressive motif. It may trace a route, connect a proof sequence, or frame a section transition. It is restrained, static by default, and red/ember only at moments of emphasis. Its purpose is to make the experience feel connected and directional; it must never become an animated flourish.

Photography is editorial and close-cropped: hands, material textures, orderly operational details, warm domestic light, and considered commercial environments. Avoid generic cylinder cut-outs, flames, trucks parked in front of offices, smiling stock call-center scenes, and busy collages. When no authentic imagery exists, use quiet abstract material compositions rather than deceptive stock claims.

## Brand expression rules

- Use red as a precise signal, not a page background.
- Allow generous neutral space; it signals confidence and gives important information room.
- Use sentence case, concrete language, and a single primary action at a time.
- Favor proof, process, and practical guidance over awards, badges, or superlatives.
- The brand must remain recognisable in an all-neutral layout; color is an accent, not the identity.
