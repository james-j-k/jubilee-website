# Component Library

| Component | User decision it supports | Inclusion rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Header and mobile navigation | Where am I, and how do I get help? | Always present; mobile retains direct call access. |
| Hero | Is this a credible service for me? | One promise, one primary action, one supporting path. |
| Reliability System | How does dependable support work? | Use only with operations-approved process details. |
| Evidence module | Why should I believe the promise? | Render only verified metrics, accreditation, or attributed customer proof. |
| Service selector | Which household need applies to me? | Two to four outcome-led choices; no catalogue dump. |
| Commercial feature | Is this designed for my operation? | Always a distinct narrative branch, not a reused household card. |
| Safety guidance | What should I do safely now? | Task-led and available from every route via navigation/footer. |
| Contact panel and form | What is the lowest-friction next step? | Direct call details first; form second. |
| Footer | Where can I recover or verify details? | Contains routes, support information, safety, and approved legal details. |

Testimonials and statistics are not default components. They are evidence variants, permitted only when their source, date, and approval are documented.
