# Visual Language Specification — Jubilee Indane Home

## 1. The central idea: Local infrastructure, made human

Jubilee Indane Home should feel like the most dependable institution in Pala: familiar enough to belong to the town, disciplined enough to be trusted with something essential.

The design language is called **Grounded Continuity**.

It combines three ideas:

1. **Provenance:** official IndianOil/Indane recognition is handled with exactness and restraint.
2. **Terrain:** the quiet contour, slope, and layered depth of the local landscape becomes a Jubilee-owned visual signature.
3. **Care:** authentic people, spaces, and service details make the experience feel lived-in rather than corporate.

The intended reaction is not “this looks like an LPG website.” It is: **“This is Jubilee. This is Pala. These people have this handled.”**

## 2. Brand ownership hierarchy

| Layer | Role | Visual treatment | What it must never become |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| IndianOil | Institutional trust | Official unmodified asset on a calm solid-white endorsement surface. | A decorative page motif or the dominant hero brand. |
| Indane | Recognised LPG ecosystem | Official unmodified asset, placed beside or below IndianOil only where approved. | A recoloured wordmark, a custom logo lockup, or a red page theme. |
| Jubilee Indane Home | Local identity and choice | Large, repeated name; own typography, material system, route line, imagery, and CTA language. | A small distributor label beneath the parent brands. |

**Rule:** IndianOil and Indane are read first as proof. Jubilee Indane Home is remembered last as the choice.

Official marks are never redrawn, masked, animated, blurred, recoloured, placed on glass, or integrated into a Jubilee symbol. Their original approval files and usage requirements supersede every rule in this document.

## 3. Photography direction — proof of place, not stock aspiration

### Purpose

Photography carries emotional credibility. It proves Jubilee is a real institution with a place, people, and standard—not a generic distributor assembled from templates.

### The photography system

Use only commissioned or approved authentic imagery from these five subject families:

| Family | Subjects | Emotional job | Composition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pala context | Hills, rain-softened streets, familiar edges of town, approved local landmarks. | Belonging and geographic memory. | Wide, quiet, unhurried; no postcard treatment. |
| Jubilee place | Agency facade, office threshold, service counter, signage, organised work surface. | Establishment and accountability. | Straight, architectural, materially specific. |
| Jubilee people | Agency team, founder/family with permission, real working gestures. | Humanity and responsibility. | Documentary, never posed as stock “customer service.” |
| Service detail | Approved paperwork, a carefully maintained workspace, safe relevant equipment detail. | Professionalism through care. | Close crop, natural texture, no staged dashboard. |
| Community life | Real Pala homes and businesses with consent. | Pride and relevance. | Observational; the subject is daily life, not a promotional event. |

### Art direction

- Light is natural, soft, and directional: early morning, overcast brightness, or warm late afternoon. Avoid flash, saturated HDR, and clinical white balance.
- Subjects are not asked to smile into camera unless the image is an intentional formal portrait.
- Use environmental negative space for typography; never burn copy into photography.
- Preserve real material imperfections: worn paint, paper grain, steel, rain, masonry, foliage, and human hands. These communicate longevity more effectively than polished stock surfaces.
- Retouch for clarity, not perfection. Do not erase the reality of place.

### Never use

- Stock families, stock delivery people, stock call centres, or staged handshakes.
- Isolated LPG cylinders, flame graphics, truck hero shots, gas-pump imagery, or montage collages.
- Images that imply Jubilee owns a facility, vehicle, customer, or service area without approval.
- AI-generated “local” people or landmarks. Local pride must be real.

## 4. The Pala Terrain signature

### The motif

Jubilee owns a restrained **terrain line**: a set of one to three thin, imperfectly spaced contour paths inspired by Pala’s hills and roads. It is not a map, route tracker, or navigation system.

The terrain line is an identity texture that quietly connects local place to operational continuity.

### Permitted use

- As a low-contrast transition between major narrative chapters.
- Cropped inside a Jubilee-owned image field or mineral surface.
- As the route line in the Jubilee Standard object, terminating in service labels rather than geographic pins.
- As a subtle embossed/debossed pattern in print-like material surfaces.

### Prohibited use

- A literal Pala map outline unless created from licensed, accurate source data and approved as an artistic—not navigational—representation.
- Google Maps styling, GIS markers, roads, delivery radii, animated live tracking, or faux location intelligence.
- Repeating contours as a background wallpaper across all pages.

### Why it works

The terrain signature is not borrowed from IndianOil or Indane. It lets Jubilee become recognisable by place and sensibility even when the logo is absent.

## 5. Illustration style

Illustration is secondary to photography and must never pretend to be a literal depiction of people or facilities.

- Use abstract, topographic, and material diagrams only.
- Build illustrations from fine graphite lines, one Jubilee-red connection, and sparse ember endpoints.
- Shapes are open, calm, and tactile—not cartoonish, isometric, glossy 3D, or corporate vector character art.
- Illustrations explain relationships: a support path, a safety sequence, or a section transition.
- Use one illustration idea per major page at most.

An illustration must answer a question. If photography can answer it more honestly, use photography.

## 6. Iconography

### Character

Icons are drafted tools, not decorations: precise, familiar, slightly softened, and structurally consistent.

- 1.5px to 1.75px stroke at the standard 24px size.
- Rounded line caps and joins.
- Grid: 24px; default optical bounds: 20px square within the grid.
- Use graphite by default. Jubilee red is reserved for the active state, a single connection point, or safety-critical emphasis.
- Pair every non-obvious icon with a text label.

### Do not use

- Flames, cylinders, trucks, generic shields, stars, checkmark clouds, emoji, or oversized category illustrations as visual filler.
- Filled blue/red corporate glyphs inherited from an unrelated icon library.
- More than one icon per compact content block.

## 7. Geometry and shape language

### Core forms

| Form | Meaning | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nameplate rectangle | Institution, certainty, documentation. | Jubilee lockup, factual labels, proof metadata. |
| Soft rectangle | Human care without softness overload. | Content panels, image frames, forms. |
| Route curve | Continuity and local terrain. | Jubilee-owned process or section connector. |
| Circle point | Moment of service/contact. | One route node, active marker, limited status cue. |

### Border-radius system

- `6px`: small labels and compact controls.
- `12px`: standard controls, cards, and image frames.
- `20px`: primary editorial panels and the Jubilee Standard plaque.
- `999px`: CTA pills and only genuinely compact metadata chips.

Avoid excessive pills. Rounded geometry should soften an institutional system, not make it look like a consumer app.

### Borders

- Use 1px neutral graphite at 8–12% opacity for structure.
- Use white borders only on the Jubilee glass plaque.
- Use a single 1px Jubilee-red rule when marking a live section transition or high-priority state.
- Never use heavy red frames, double borders, dashed ornaments, or decorative rules.

## 8. Materials, glass, texture, and shadow

### Mineral canvas

The base field is a warm, near-white mineral canvas (`#F8F7F4`), not pure white. It has enough warmth to feel local and human, while retaining the engineering clarity of a product interface.

### Paper grain

A nearly imperceptible grain may sit on the mineral canvas or an editorial stage.

- Coverage: below 18% opacity.
- Scale: 4–6px.
- It is static, never animated.
- It must disappear for reduced-transparency environments or when it reduces text contrast.

### Glass

Glass belongs only to **Jubilee-owned proof or standard objects**.

- One major glass object per viewport.
- Mineral-white surface at 44–58% opacity.
- Backdrop blur: 14–18px; saturation: 105–115% maximum.
- 1px white border at 60–72% opacity.
- One low, diffuse graphite shadow; no rainbow refraction or liquid-glass distortion.
- Official IndianOil and Indane assets never sit on glass.

### Shadows

Shadows explain elevation, not luxury.

| Elevation | Shadow | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Resting panel | `0 8px 20px rgb(17 24 39 / 7%)` | Standard material separation. |
| Editorial plaque | `0 22px 48px rgb(17 24 39 / 11%)` | One focal Jubilee-owned object. |
| CTA hover | `0 11px 23px rgb(17 24 39 / 17%)` | Interaction feedback only. |

Never stack multiple shadows or use coloured drop shadows except the low-opacity Jubilee-red halo behind a focal plaque.

## 9. Color psychology and composition

### Jubilee-owned palette

| Token | Value | Psychological role | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mineral | `#F8F7F4` | Calm, openness, human warmth. | Page canvas. |
| Graphite | `#111827` | Competence, authority, readability. | Headings, CTA, structural text. |
| Slate | `#596170` | Measured explanation. | Long-form body copy. |
| Jubilee red | `#D72638` | Agency-owned action and continuity. | Fine rules, route origin, active/focus state. |
| Ember | `#F97316` | Resolution and warmth. | Route endpoint only. |
| Cloud | `#FFFFFF` | Clean factual surface. | Endorsement rail and panels. |

### Institutional palette boundary

IndianOil’s official logo colors are used only in official, unmodified IndianOil artwork. The official IndianOil guidance identifies Orange 1655 C and Blue 2757 C for true logo reproduction. Do not approximate or extend these colors into Jubilee interface gradients, buttons, or illustrations. [IndianOil corporate logo guidance](https://iocl.com/corporate-logo)

Use the official Indane asset unchanged after written approval. Do not infer an Indane color palette from cylinder imagery or unofficial logo files.

### Page-level allocation

- 65% mineral/off-white.
- 18% graphite/slate typography and structure.
- 10% cloud/surface.
- 4% Jubilee red.
- 2% official IndianOil/Indane asset presence where required.
- 1% ember.

Scarcity makes color credible. Red and orange should guide attention, never create urgency theatre.

## 10. Typography philosophy

Geist is the sole interface and editorial family. It makes Jubilee feel contemporary without borrowing a luxury serif identity that would weaken the operational tone.

### Typographic voice

- **Jubilee name:** large, quiet, slightly tight tracking; always the biggest proper noun on a page.
- **Official relationship text:** smaller, factual, and never more visually prominent than Jubilee.
- **Headlines:** sentence case, outcome-led, no slogan stacking.
- **Body:** short paragraphs, ordinary words, direct verbs.
- **Metadata:** modest uppercase only when a document-like label is genuinely useful.

### Hierarchy behavior

The page begins broad and editorial, then becomes progressively more practical:

```text
Identity → local story → service standard → route selection → safety → contact
```

This shifts the reader from emotional recognition to a concrete decision without an abrupt change in voice.

### Copy rules

- Say `Jubilee Indane Home` in every primary conversion moment.
- Say `Authorised IndianOil Indane Distributor` only where the relationship needs clarification; never repeat it mechanically.
- Prefer `Speak to Jubilee` and `Contact Jubilee Indane Home` to generic labels.
- Do not use “best,” “leading,” “world-class,” “trusted by thousands,” or “seamless” without verifiable evidence.

## 11. Spacing and white-space strategy

White space is how Jubilee communicates it is not scrambling for attention.

### Spacing scale

`4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96, 128px`

No one-off spacing values are permitted without a documented visual reason.

### Page rhythm

- Major editorial stages: 80px mobile / 128px desktop vertical padding.
- Structured information panels: 56px mobile / 96px desktop vertical padding.
- Headline to support: 16–24px.
- Support to action: 24–32px.
- Panel interior: 20–24px mobile / 32px desktop.

### Strategic emptiness

Reserve unoccupied space in three places:

1. Around the Jubilee name in the Hero.
2. Between the local story and the Jubilee Difference.
3. Before the final contact chapter.

These pauses let the visitor absorb trust before being asked to process more information. Empty space is never a substitute for missing content; it is a pacing device.

## 12. Grid and component proportions

### Grid

| Viewport | Grid | Gutter | Container |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 320–479px | 4 columns | 20px | Fluid |
| 480–767px | 4 columns | 24px | Fluid |
| 768–1279px | 8 columns | 32–48px | Fluid |
| 1280–1599px | 12 columns | 48px | 1200px max |
| 1600–1920px | 12 columns | 80px | 1440px max |

### Component proportions

- Primary CTA: 48px minimum mobile height, 52px desktop height; natural width except at ≤359px where it is full-width.
- Standard content panel: radius 12px, 1px structural border, no shadow unless it is a material layer.
- Jubilee editorial plaque: 20px radius, one glass surface, 5:4 desktop or 4:3 mobile.
- Image field: 4:5 mobile or 5:4 desktop, never a shallow decorative banner.
- Service routes: editorial split, not a grid of equal “feature cards.”

## 13. Visual rhythm

The homepage alternates between two compositional modes.

### Open stage

Used for the Hero, Pala community story, commercial proposition, and final contact invitation.

- Broad mineral canvas.
- Strong editorial type.
- One compelling image or material object.
- Deliberate asymmetry on desktop.

### Structured panel

Used for the Jubilee Difference, support routing, safety gateway, and proof.

- Higher information density.
- Precise grid and clear boundaries.
- Minimal image use.
- Content grouped by the customer decision it supports.

Alternate open stage → structured panel → open stage. This makes the page feel paced like a story rather than a feed of cards.

## 14. Motion philosophy

Motion should make Jubilee feel organised and calm.

### Allowed motion

- One-time 180–420ms fade/settle for entering content.
- One 420ms route-line draw to explain a verified sequence or service split.
- 160ms CTA press/hover feedback.
- Short menu or modal state transitions that preserve context.

### Prohibited motion

- Infinite floating, pulsing, scrolling text, number count-ups, parallax, cursor trails, autoplay video, and kinetic logo treatment.
- Any motion applied to official IndianOil or Indane marks.
- Animation that delays safety, contact, or locality information.

### Reduced motion

The final state is always complete before motion. With `prefers-reduced-motion`, all nonessential animation resolves immediately; content is never hidden behind a transition.

## 15. Responsive philosophy

Mobile is the authoritative composition, not a compressed desktop.

- Identity → explanation → action always stays in that order.
- Copy never overlays a busy image on mobile; it appears above or below it.
- Official marks remain legible and horizontal; they never turn into a dense badge stack.
- Visual texture compresses or crops before type, action, or locality context does.
- The mobile CTA appears before a major visual object whenever the visitor may need urgent support.
- Desktop adds editorial space and asymmetry, not more content or more calls to action.
- At 200% zoom, desktop may reflow to the mobile narrative order without losing controls or text.

## 16. The emotional local chapter: Proudly Serving Pala

This is the evolution of “Local Accountability.” It is not an About section.

### Purpose

Give Pala residents an honest moment of recognition and pride: **“Yes, that is our agency.”**

### Required content

- Heading: `Proudly serving Pala.`
- One real image from the approved photography system.
- One real, approved agency or community fact.
- Optional terrain line as a low-contrast compositional element.
- One quiet link: `Meet Jubilee` or `Find Jubilee`.

### Constraints

- Never manufacture nostalgia or use a generic “community” paragraph.
- Never use an inaccurate Pala map outline, a tourism postcard, or an unverified historical claim.
- Keep the focus on Jubilee’s relationship to Pala, not IndianOil’s national scale.

This chapter is the emotional hinge of the homepage: the Hero makes Jubilee credible; Pala makes Jubilee personal.

## 17. Acceptance test: recognisable without the logo

A future page follows this visual language only if, after concealing the official and Jubilee logos, it still feels like Jubilee because it contains:

- Mineral canvas, graphite structure, and scarce Jubilee-red continuity.
- An open-stage / structured-panel rhythm.
- Grounded local photography or terrain-line materiality.
- Calm, large Geist typography with direct language.
- One carefully used glass material object, never glass everywhere.
- Geometry that balances institutional certainty with local warmth.
- Motion that explains order rather than seeking attention.

If a page could belong equally to a generic insurer, food-delivery startup, gas company, or local directory, it does not yet belong to Jubilee Indane Home.
