# Hero Specification — Premium Infrastructure

## 0. Purpose and non-negotiables

The hero must make **Jubilee Indane Home** the memorable answer to “Who should I trust for LPG support?” It is not an abstract energy campaign and not a generic local-business banner. It presents the agency as composed, established, local infrastructure for everyday life.

The desired three-second conclusion is: **“Jubilee Indane Home is the dependable agency for my home or business; I can contact them directly.”**

### Claim gate

No metric, tenure, authorisation statement, service-area promise, response time, safety certification, testimonial, or availability claim may appear until an approved source, owner, and review date exist. The design must remain complete when these inputs are absent. Truthfulness is a trust mechanism, not a content limitation.

### Required approved inputs before final content lock

| Input | Hero use | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Exact agency lockup | `Jubilee Indane Home`, including approved Indane usage | Brand/legal owner |
| Primary service locality | Context line and visual location marker | Operations |
| Direct contact route | Primary CTA destination; phone only if approved | Operations |
| One verified proof item | Optional evidence capsule | Operations/marketing |
| One authentic operational image | Visual texture or proof object background | Brand/content owner |

## 1. Information hierarchy

| Order | Element | Exact content rule | Why it exists / business goal / psychological principle |
| ---: | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Agency lockup | `Jubilee Indane Home` appears in the top-left at every viewport, and once again inside the hero headline. | Establishes the proper noun before a generic category. Goal: brand recall and direct enquiries. Principle: repetition with spatial consistency creates recognition without feeling promotional. |
| 2 | Primary promise | `Reliable LPG support. From Jubilee Indane Home.` | Answers relevance and trust in plain language before stylistic expression. Goal: reduce bounce and comparison shopping. Principle: processing fluency—an immediately understood message feels more credible. |
| 3 | Locality context | `For homes and businesses in [approved locality].` | Connects an otherwise abstract service to the visitor’s real world. Goal: qualify relevant visitors. Principle: proximity and in-group recognition increase perceived accountability. |
| 4 | Primary CTA | `Contact Jubilee Indane Home` | Converts attention at the moment confidence is highest. Goal: start the correct support conversation. Principle: one clear action lowers decision paralysis. |
| 5 | Service-standard visual | A physical-looking “Jubilee Standard” plaque and a restrained local energy route. | Makes the agency feel operationally organised without inventing a dashboard. Goal: create reliability perception. Principle: deliberate structure and material cues signal competence. |
| 6 | Conditional proof capsule | A single verified fact, e.g. approved tenure or authorised relationship; omitted when unavailable. | Adds proof only when it can carry real weight. Goal: reinforce, never fabricate, credibility. Principle: specificity is more persuasive than a pile of vague signals. |

Do not add navigation, a second CTA, a testimonial strip, a metrics row, a safety section, or a scroll prompt inside this hero. They compete with the first decision or imply unsupported content below.

## 2. Exact layout

### Mobile: 320–767px

```text
┌─────────────────────────────────────┐
│ [J] Jubilee Indane Home              │  20px outer gutter
│     Local LPG support                 │
│                                     │
│ LPG support for [approved locality] │
│                                     │
│ Reliable LPG support.                │
│ From Jubilee Indane Home.            │
│                                     │
│ For homes and businesses that rely   │
│ on clear, considered support.        │
│                                     │
│ [ Contact Jubilee Indane Home  ↗ ]  │
│                                     │
│ ┌─────────────────────────────────┐ │
│ │  Jubilee Standard               │ │
│ │  ─── local energy route ───     │ │
│ │  [approved proof, if available] │ │
│ └─────────────────────────────────┘ │
└─────────────────────────────────────┘
```

- **Header lockup:** 32px minimum high touch-safe region, 20px page gutter at 320px, 24px at 480px+. It is not a full site navigation in this phase.
- **Copy starts:** 72px below the lockup. This creates an intentional pause without pushing the agency name off screen.
- **Headline width:** 8–10 words across two to four natural lines; never manually broken for a device width.
- **CTA:** full-width at ≤359px; content-width pill at ≥360px. It appears before the visual object so a visitor can act without scrolling.
- **Visual object:** follows the CTA with 32px gap. It is a 4:3 rounded panel, never taller than 370px on standard mobile. The visual compresses before the copy does.
- **No persistent bottom CTA:** it would obscure the plaque and make the experience feel coercive.

**Why:** Mobile visitors often arrive with an immediate practical need. Agency identity, relevance, and action must fit the first view. The visual earns attention after the decision path is clear.

**Business goal:** Increase qualified contact intent without making people hunt for the agency identity.

**Psychology:** The serial-position effect prioritises first information; placing the agency name and exact service category first anchors the rest of the experience.

### Desktop: 768–1920px

```text
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ [J] Jubilee Indane Home                                  [contact action] │
│     Local LPG support                                                     │
│                                                                          │
│  LPG support for [approved locality]          ┌────────────────────────┐ │
│                                                │ Jubilee Standard       │ │
│  Reliable LPG support.                         │                        │ │
│  From Jubilee Indane Home.                     │   physical route /     │ │
│                                                │   authentic operational │ │
│  Clear support for homes and businesses.       │   material detail      │ │
│                                                │                        │ │
│  [ Contact Jubilee Indane Home  ↗ ]           │ [approved proof only] │ │
│                                                └────────────────────────┘ │
│                                                                          │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

- **Container:** 1200px maximum content width at 1280px+; 1440px maximum at 1600px+; 48px to 80px horizontal page gutter depending on viewport.
- **Grid:** 12 columns. Copy occupies columns 1–6; visual occupies columns 8–12; column 7 is intentional breathing room. Do not fill this gap.
- **Vertical placement:** hero content is vertically centred within a minimum 760px desktop hero, with 96px minimum header clearance.
- **Copy measure:** maximum 520px. Keeping the sentence measure compact preserves impact beside a substantial visual object.
- **Visual panel:** ratio 5:4, constrained to 560px × 448px at the intended desktop size. At 1440px+, it may reach 640px × 512px. It must never become wider than the copy’s visual authority.
- **Ultra-wide 1920px:** retain the maximum content frame and grow outer negative space. Do not stretch the grid or type to fill the screen.

**Why:** The asymmetry lets copy establish certainty while the visual quietly substantiates it. Empty space makes the agency feel confident, rather than under-designed.

**Business goal:** Present Jubilee Indane Home as a serious, stable institution to both household and commercial visitors.

**Psychology:** A clear visual hierarchy reduces cognitive load. Deliberate white space and aligned geometry are read as organisational control.

## 3. Typography scale

Geist is the only typeface. Use its variable axes conservatively; no decorative font pairing.

| Role | 320–767px | 768–1439px | 1440–1920px | Weight / line height | Rule and rationale |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- | --- |
| Agency lockup | 17px | 18px | 19px | 600 / 1.05 | The name is compact but unmistakable. It is a brand signature, not interface chrome. |
| Descriptor/locality | 12px | 13px | 13px | 500 / 1.35 | Gives context without competing with the name. |
| Eyebrow | 12px | 13px | 13px | 600 / 1.35 | Uses sentence case; never all caps. It helps visitors classify the service quickly. |
| H1 | 46px | 66px | 80px | 540 / 0.98 | The promise feels authoritative through proportion, not heavy weight. Use -0.055em to -0.065em tracking. |
| Body | 17px | 18px | 19px | 400 / 1.5 | Gives service context in an easy-to-scan measure. |
| CTA | 15px | 15px | 16px | 600 / 1 | Keeps the action precise and clearly tappable. |
| Plaque title | 11px | 12px | 12px | 650 / 1.25 | Functional label, not a marketing eyebrow. |
| Plaque value | 20px | 24px | 26px | 560 / 1.08 | A concise reliability idea or approved proof. |

The headline must be text, never embedded in an image. The agency name receives one full line within the H1: `From Jubilee Indane Home.` This is non-negotiable for recall.

**Why:** Large, quiet typography acts as confidence; a physical service should not need visual shouting.

**Business goal:** Make the agency’s proper name memorable before visitors compare vendors.

**Psychology:** Fluency and repetition increase familiarity; familiar signals are more likely to be judged safe and trustworthy.

## 4. CTA placement and behavior

### Primary CTA

- Label: `Contact Jubilee Indane Home`
- Destination: approved contact route only.
- Placement: directly after the body copy; never below the visual.
- Mobile: minimum 48px tall; full width under 360px.
- Desktop: 52px tall; natural-content width; never paired with a visually equal action.
- Color: graphite fill with white text. Indane red remains a focus and brand signal, not an aggressive conversion field.

### Header action

At desktop width only, a low-emphasis text link, `Contact`, may appear in the upper-right and point to the same route. It is a recovery path for visitors who scan navigation first; the in-content CTA remains the dominant action. On mobile, omit it until an approved direct phone number is available.

**Why:** One primary decision keeps an urgent visitor moving. The same contact route is available in two visual contexts on desktop without presenting two different choices.

**Business goal:** Increase contact intent and reduce abandonment.

**Psychology:** Hick’s Law—fewer comparable choices decrease decision time. Clear verb-object labels lower uncertainty about the next state.

## 5. Trust indicators

### Always present

1. **Full agency name:** `Jubilee Indane Home` in the lockup and headline.
2. **Service category:** `LPG support` written in plain language.
3. **Fit statement:** `For homes and businesses in [approved locality].`
4. **Composed operational visual:** the Jubilee Standard plaque and route visual, described below.

### Conditional proof capsule

One proof item only. It sits at the bottom of the visual panel and follows this exact pattern:

```text
[verified value]  [specific label]
Source/approval available in content record
```

Allowed examples after approval:

- `[X] years` serving `[locality]`
- `Authorised [relationship]` with exact approved wording
- An attributed customer statement under 18 words, with permission

Disallowed: star ratings without source, logo walls, “trusted by thousands,” fake live status, fabricated service coverage, count-up numbers, or visual badges that cannot be verified.

**Why:** One precise proof cue carries more trust than a noisy stack of generic badges.

**Business goal:** Reduce vendor-comparison behaviour and improve enquiry confidence.

**Psychology:** Specificity and sourceability signal accountability. Over-signalling is interpreted as persuasion and can lower trust.

## 6. Visual composition

### The “Jubilee Standard” object

The right-side visual is a tactile, semi-translucent operational plaque—not a dashboard, delivery map, or cylinder illustration.

```text
┌─────────────────────────────────┐
│ JUBILEE INDANE HOME              │
│ The Jubilee Standard             │
│                                 │
│    ╲___ restrained red route ___ │
│      • Home       • Commercial   │
│                                 │
│ Start with clarity.              │
│ [single approved proof capsule] │
└─────────────────────────────────┘
```

- **Background layer:** one authentic, close-cropped operational material image—organised paperwork, a safe service environment, an authentic agency detail, or an approved local landmark. It may never imply a customer, driver, vehicle, or facility that is not real.
- **Foreground plaque:** translucent mineral-white glass. The readable content layer must stay in the top half; visual route sits in the lower half.
- **Energy route:** a single red-to-ember line crossing the panel and terminating at two labelled nodes: `Home` and `Commercial`. It represents the two supported visitor paths, not live tracking or actual delivery geography.
- **Location marker:** one small locality label if approved. Never display an inaccurate map pin.
- **No more than one proof capsule:** it is either present with real proof or the space is intentionally blank.

**Why:** It gives the service physicality and system discipline without pretending the agency is a technology platform.

**Business goal:** Convey that Jubilee Indane Home is established, prepared, and designed around customer needs.

**Psychology:** Materiality, order, and restrained information density are interpreted as competence. Familiar category labels help users self-identify quickly.

## 7. Color composition

| Role | Color | Approximate visual share | Use |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| Mineral canvas | `#F8F7F4` | 62% | Hero field and room around the agency name. |
| Graphite | `#111827` | 18% | Headline, CTA, and structural confidence. |
| Soft surface | `#FFFFFF` with transparency | 14% | Plaque and quiet material elevation. |
| Jubilee/Indane red | `#D72638` | 4% | Agency mark accent, energy line origin, focus state. |
| Ember | `#F97316` | 2% | Energy-line destination only. |

- Red must not fill the hero, plaque, or CTA. Its scarcity gives it authority.
- Orange must not become a second brand or CTA color.
- Use a subtle red halo behind the plaque only when it improves separation; opacity stays under 10%.
- Ensure all body and interactive text meets WCAG AA contrast against its final composited surface.

**Why:** A neutral infrastructure palette prevents LPG red from reading as promotional or alarmist.

**Business goal:** Differentiate Jubilee Indane Home as premium and controlled while retaining immediate category recognition.

**Psychology:** Restrained high-saturation color becomes a signal of importance; overuse becomes visual pressure.

## 8. Glassmorphism usage

Glass is a material cue, not the design language.

- Use exactly one major glass surface: the Jubilee Standard plaque.
- Surface: 44–58% white transparency over a deliberately simple background.
- Blur: 14–18px maximum; saturation increase 105–115% maximum.
- Border: 1px white at 60–72% opacity, with a subtle darker outer shadow for separation.
- Internal contrast: never place low-contrast text over a busy crop. Add an internal opaque-to-transparent reading gradient if the image requires it.
- Do not glassify the header, CTA, chips, navigation, or every node.

**Why:** One material object communicates care and modernity. Repeated frosted panels look trend-driven and dilute information hierarchy.

**Business goal:** Create a premium recognisable visual moment while keeping the agency name and CTA dominant.

**Psychology:** Depth cues create perceived craft, but only when they clarify layer relationships.

## 9. Motion timeline

The static first frame must be complete and readable. Motion only explains the relationship between the agency, the plaque, and the contact path.

| Time | Motion | Duration | Rule / rationale |
| ---: | --- | ---: | --- |
| 0ms | Full final-state content is present in DOM. | — | Prevents motion from hiding essential trust information. |
| 0–180ms | Agency lockup fades in with 8px upward settle. | 180ms | The name arrives first, reinforcing brand primacy. |
| 80–420ms | Eyebrow, H1, body, CTA enter as one staggered copy group. | 340ms | Establishes reading order without a theatrical word-by-word reveal. |
| 150–520ms | Plaque fades/scales from 98% to 100%. | 370ms | Gives the operational object material weight. |
| 420–840ms | Energy route draws once, left to right. | 420ms | Communicates continuity between the two support paths. |
| 580–820ms | `Home` and `Commercial` nodes appear. | 240ms | Labels become visible only after the route relationship is legible. |
| Hover/focus | CTA lifts 1–2px and arrow translates 2px. | 160ms | Confirms action without visual noise. |

- Easing: `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` for entrances; standard ease-out for CTA feedback.
- No parallax, cursor-following, looping pulse, count-up, animated particles, or autonomous float.
- `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` renders final states immediately and disables all nonessential transitions.

**Why:** The sequence reinforces “name → promise → proof object → support paths.”

**Business goal:** Increase comprehension and perceived operational calm without delaying conversion.

**Psychology:** Ordered reveal leverages continuity; movement that maps to a meaning is easier to trust than decorative motion.

## 10. Accessibility considerations

- Use one H1 containing the full primary promise and agency name.
- Agency lockup links to home and has an explicit accessible name: `Jubilee Indane Home home`.
- CTA is a semantic link when it navigates and a semantic button only when it opens an in-page contact mechanism.
- CTA target: minimum 48 × 48px mobile, 44 × 44px desktop minimum.
- Energy route, nodes, image texture, and decorative glass treatments are `aria-hidden`; no information required to act appears only there.
- Body text remains at least 16px; labels remain at least 12px with sufficient contrast and are never the only carrier of essential meaning.
- Keyboard focus is a 2px visible ring with at least 3:1 contrast against both light and glass surfaces.
- No automatic motion is necessary to understand content. Reduced motion is tested as a complete visual state.
- At 200% browser zoom, copy stays above the plaque and no CTA or lockup is clipped.

**Why:** Trust falls immediately when an essential service is difficult to read, tap, or control.

**Business goal:** Keep the contact route open to all users, including visitors under stress or using assistive technology.

**Psychology:** Predictability and user control reduce anxiety, especially in service interactions.

## 11. Performance considerations

- Render the text, CTA, and agency name as server-rendered HTML; no client JavaScript is required for initial comprehension.
- Use one hero image maximum. It must have `width`, `height`, responsive `sizes`, AVIF/WebP output, and an intentional crop. Use no image if an approved authentic image is unavailable; the mineral material composition remains the fallback.
- Prefer CSS and a tiny inline SVG for the route. Do not load a canvas, WebGL scene, Lottie runtime, video, or animation library for this hero.
- Animate only `opacity` and `transform`; SVG stroke drawing is acceptable once. Avoid layout, filter, and background-position animation.
- Blur applies only to the one plaque and is disabled or reduced on low-end/mobile environments if testing shows paint cost.
- Keep hero-specific client JavaScript at 0KB if CSS motion suffices. The hero should not become a client component solely for aesthetics.
- Target: hero LCP content is visible without waiting for a decorative asset; no layout shift after first render.

**Why:** Fast rendering is a reliability cue; a late or shifting hero breaks the promise before a user reads it.

**Business goal:** Preserve conversion on mobile networks and improve search performance.

**Psychology:** Response speed shapes perceived competence and reduces uncertainty.

## 12. Empty, unavailable, and loading states

### No approved image

Render the plaque over the mineral canvas with subtle paper/grain texture and no fake photography. Keep the route, title, and agency lockup. Do not use a random stock image.

### No approved locality

Use `For homes and businesses.` Do not imply geographical service coverage.

### No verified proof

Omit the proof capsule entirely. The plaque should retain its visual balance through empty lower space; do not replace it with an unverified badge.

### Contact route unavailable

Do not show a disabled CTA. A disabled primary action communicates that the agency is unavailable. Instead, the hero cannot launch until an approved contact destination exists. In staging only, use a clearly labelled internal placeholder.

### Image loading

Reserve the final image aspect ratio from first paint. Use a mineral-coloured base with the plaque already visible; do not use a spinner. Image arrival may be a 150ms opacity fade only if it does not delay text or CTA.

### Motion unavailable

Final visual state is the default state. The route is fully drawn and labels are visible; the hero never appears incomplete.

**Why:** Missing business inputs are normal during production; the experience must fail gracefully without making false promises.

**Business goal:** Avoid reputational damage and lost enquiries caused by placeholders, broken actions, or fake trust signals.

**Psychology:** A calm, intentional absence is more trustworthy than an obviously improvised substitute.
