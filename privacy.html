Privacy

Privacy policy

What happens to information you choose to share with Jubilee Indane Home through this website, in plain terms.

Last updated 28 September 2026

Information you choose to share

You may share your name, phone number, consumer number, business details or an enquiry when you call us, email us or message us on WhatsApp.

Buttons on this website such as “Book on WhatsApp” open WhatsApp with a pre-filled message. Nothing is sent until you press send in WhatsApp.

Refill bookings for household cylinders use IndianOil’s official Indane booking channels (WhatsApp 75888 88824 and missed call 84549 55555). Those services are run by IndianOil, and its own privacy terms apply.

The refill planner and safety checklist

The refill planner and gas-safety checklist work entirely in your browser. Nothing you enter is sent to Jubilee Indane Home.

So the planner remembers you next time, your cylinder size and dates are saved only in this browser on your device. Use “Forget my details” in the planner to remove them at any time.

Your language choice (English or Malayalam) is saved the same way.

“Add a reminder” creates a calendar file on your own device. It is not uploaded anywhere.

Installing the website as an app

If you add Jubilee Indane Home to your home screen, a copy of the website’s pages and files is kept on your device so it opens quickly and works offline. No personal information is sent to us.

Website analytics

We use Vercel Web Analytics to count visits and see which pages are viewed, as totals. It does not use cookies and does not identify you personally.

Hosting

This website is hosted by Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel processes standard technical information, such as your IP address and browser type, to deliver pages securely.

How we use your information

We use the information you give us to answer your LPG enquiry, arrange refills or connections, and provide the support you asked for. We do not use it for anything else.

Sharing

Jubilee Indane Home does not sell your personal information.

When you contact us by WhatsApp or email, your message passes through that service, and its own privacy terms apply.

Keeping information safe

We take reasonable care with information you share with us. Please don’t send unnecessary sensitive information, such as identity documents, through WhatsApp or email unless we ask for it for your connection.

Questions and updates

For any question about this policy, call +91 94470 71889 or email jubileeindanehome@gmail.com.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page.

Safe. Reliable. Convenient.
